# Living Workshop

Welcome. The workshop works best when each contributor brings one small, useful change.

## Choose one deliverable

Pick a task you can describe in a sentence and complete as a draft. Keep it small enough for someone to review without needing to understand the whole project. Before you begin, say what done will look like. Name an observable check, such as a file containing a particular heading or a page showing a specific instruction.

For example: **Add a short note to the contributor guide explaining where to report a confusing instruction.** Done means the note names the place to report it and gives one clear sentence to use. A reviewer can open the guide and see both pieces of information.

## Bring back a draft

When your draft is ready, bring it to the workshop and ask a human to review it. Say what you changed, point to the check that shows the task is done, and mention anything you are unsure about. The reviewer can suggest a small adjustment, and you can revise the draft before calling the task complete.
